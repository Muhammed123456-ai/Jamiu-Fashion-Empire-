Do You Have A Question?

Common Questions Answered • Zero Guesswork.

Everything you need to know about our remote measurements, materials, split payment, and delivery logistics.

1 How do you get my accurate measurements if I live outside Lokoja or cannot visit Felele?

You do not need a measuring tape or a physical visit. Through our proven MeasurementMatch system, Jamiu estimates your exact chest, shoulder, sleeve, and trouser proportions from two clear full-length photos of your body standing straight.

Before cutting any material, Jamiu confirms each dimension with you over WhatsApp or a quick 2-minute video call. Over 60% of our clients in Abuja, Lagos, and campus executives order remotely with a 100% confirmed fit on arrival.

2 Can I bring my own material or wedding Aso-Ebi for Jamiu to sew?

Yes, absolutely. While Jamiu specializes in complete packages where we source 100% quality Cashmere, Wool, Senator materials, and Atiku, we regularly cut and sew client-supplied Aso-Ebi for weddings, family ceremonies, graduations, and titles.

You can drop off your material at our Felele Bus Stop workshop in Lokoja or waybill it to us from anywhere in Nigeria. You receive the exact same 10-day delivery guarantee and split-payment protection.

3 What happens if my clothe does not fit perfectly when it arrives?

You are 100% protected by our "Love It or We Fix It" Guarantee. First, you see a video of your clothe in progress halfway through sewing, plus a full showcase video of the finished outfit before final dispatch.

If anything does not match what you confirmed or the fit requires adjustment, Jamiu reworks it completely free of charge. Most importantly, you only pay the remaining 40% balance after you have inspected and approved your clothe. If you are still not satisfied, you do not pay the balance.

4 How does the 60% deposit and payment work?

We never ask for 100% upfront payment like other tailors. To secure your sewing slot and purchase your premium quality material, you make a 60% commitment deposit.

The remaining 40% balance is only paid after your clothe is finished, ironed, and approved by you via high-definition video confirmation. We accept instant bank transfers with official digital invoices issued immediately.

5 How long does sewing take, and can you handle rush orders below 10 days?

Standard production is guaranteed within 10 days from the day your deposit and measurements are confirmed—no delays, no excuses, and no "tailor stories."

For urgent events like last-minute weddings, FYB dinners, or convocation ceremonies needed within 4 to 7 days, express emergency production is available with an additional ₦5,000 express delivery fee. Contact Jamiu directly on WhatsApp immediately to lock in an emergency production slot.

6 Where is Jamiu physically located, and how does interstate waybill work?

Our master tailoring workshop is physically located at Felele Bus Stop, Lokoja, Kogi State, Nigeria. We are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM for walk-in consultations, measurements, and order pickups.

For clients outside Lokoja (Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt, and nationwide), we waybill finished clothes via trusted transport lines (GIG Logistics, Peace Mass Transit, and interstate executive parks) with direct phone tracking and waybill receipts sent directly to your WhatsApp.
